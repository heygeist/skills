# Caption Contract

The user owns the social copy. Save it verbatim and report issues; do not silently rewrite it.

## Check

- The opening and image hook support each other without repeating the same sentence.
- Claims are supported by the supplied source.
- Comparisons do not imply better or worse without evidence.
- Specialized terms are explained before they are relied upon.
- Links, mentions, hashtags, and calls to action are intentional.
- Markdown syntax will not appear literally on the target platform unless intended.
- Paragraphs scan comfortably on mobile.
- The language and tone match the stated audience and brand.
- The caption does not describe proof that the image does not show.

## Response

Classify findings as:

- **Blocker**: unsupported or contradictory claim, unsafe disclosure, or materially misleading statement
- **Suggestion**: clarity, order, mobile scanning, or tone improvement

Offer revised copy only when the user asks for it.
